# Suno CUA Advanced form observations

## 2026-09-30

The Advanced v6 form used empty Lyrics for an instrumental, and Vocal Gender had no selection. In the observed CUA session, `fill("")` left the previous lyrics in the editor. Clearing with `Meta+A` and `Backspace` allowed the adapter to verify the resulting paragraph text.

The Max Mode tooltip stated that each song cost twice as many credits. The v6 FAQ listed two songs for 10 credits per base Create, yielding an estimate of 20 credits for the no-reference Max Mode package at that check. The estimate came from the tooltip and documentation. [Suno v6 FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13924481)

## 2026-10-11

The Custom duration controls exposed a reset icon in place of an Auto choice. The adapter used that control and verified the form returned to Auto.
