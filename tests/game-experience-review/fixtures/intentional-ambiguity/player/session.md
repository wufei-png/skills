# Player-visible material: farewell-c

The client is alive and requested a farewell. The organizer knows; guests have not been told. A work note says: "Showing this note will disclose the arrangement to its recipient. Reading it here does not tell anyone."

The client's sister says she wants to speak to him before deciding whether to participate. A colleague says the client may be trying to avoid a difficult conversation. These are attributed statements, not confirmed facts.

Three actions are available:

- Delay the ceremony: no resource cost; return to the room and investigate further.
- Speak privately to the client: ask about his reasons without informing the guests.
- Stop the commission: a confirmation explains that the service ends and the client will be told; cancel returns without applying it.

The player may reopen the statements and work note at no cost. No claim is made that one decision is the correct ending.
