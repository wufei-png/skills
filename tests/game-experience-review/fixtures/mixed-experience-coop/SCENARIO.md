# Carrying a shared pencil

Plan a focused playtest of a local two-player game. One partner has substantial game experience; the other has little. The intended experience is shared contribution and enjoyable coordination, including recovery after mistakes. Both control one end of the same pencil and must align, crouch, and jump together.

The only existing player-facing material is the synthetic replay description in `player/replay.md`. It is not a human session, video, or playable build. The source version is `pencil-d`; default speed and timing assistance. No input-device or listening checks have been performed.

Use three complementary perspectives to design a short task, observations, a neutral debrief, and a decision the results could inform. Do not infer that the inexperienced partner is already excluded or that successful replay proves the experience works.
